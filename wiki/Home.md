# Advanced Equipment Reforged Wiki

Advanced Equipment Reforged adds usable equipment to Arma Reforger scenarios: laptops with a terminal and a
desktop, servers, routers, flash drives, network and power cables, generators, batteries, solar panels and a
floodlight. The pages are ordered by audience: players and Game Masters first, then mission makers, server
admins and script authors.

## Start here

- [Getting Started](Getting-Started.md): first scenario and basic concepts.
- [Player Guide](Player-Guide.md): actions, cables, flash drives, terminal and desktop in play.
- [Game Master Guide](Game-Master-Guide.md): placing devices, the action panel and attributes during a session.
- [World Editor Guide](World-Editor-Guide.md): devices, links, accounts and intel set up before the mission.
- [Server Admin Guide](Server-Admin-Guide.md): settings, mission header, persistence, dedicated servers.
