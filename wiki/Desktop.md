# Desktop

Opened with "Use desktop" or the `desktop` command. Sign-in screen (Tab: next field), wallpapers 1-5, themes
Dark / Light / Olive, notifications. Window size: setting `m_iDesktopSize`.

- Desktop icons: click selects, double click or Enter opens, right click for the menu; right click on the
  background for Refresh, Files, Terminal, Personalize, System info.
- Windows: drag by the title bar; drop on the top edge to maximize, on the left or right edge to fill that half;
  double click the title bar to maximize or restore; resize from the bottom right corner. Ctrl+W closes.
- Taskbar: start button, pinned Files / Browser / Mail / Terminal, open windows (click to focus or minimize,
  right click to close), clock. Start menu: search box (Enter opens the first match), power menu.

| Photos | Image files (`AE3_MEDIA|image|...`) |
